About this view

Values are the INCA analysis of GeoSphere Austria (stations, radar and weather model combined), not raw measurements. Times are UTC; Vienna time is shown next to them.

Cold front passage: at a point, cooling of at least 3 K in 3 h with a pressure rise of at least 2 hPa, as part of a line that crosses Austria from the west or north. Thunderstorm: at least 3 K cooling in one hour with at least 2 mm of rain. Cold drop: at least 8 K colder than 24 h before. Definitions and references

Data: GeoSphere Austria, data.hub.geosphere.at, CC BY 4.0. Map: CARTO, OpenStreetMap contributors.
